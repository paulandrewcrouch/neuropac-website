Cookies Policy

Last updated: September 2026

What are cookies?

Cookies, and similar technologies such as local storage and tracking pixels, are small files or pieces of data stored on your device by websites you visit. They can help websites function, remember your preferences, or track your activity across sites.

Cookies on this website

This website does not currently set any cookies, and does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking technologies of any kind. Our webfonts are served from our own domain rather than a third party, so no font-related request is made to an external server either. The only technical data generated when you visit is standard server log data handled by our hosting provider — see the “Technical data” and “International transfers” sections of our Privacy Policy for details.

If we introduce cookies or similar technologies in future (for example, privacy-friendly analytics to understand how the site is used), we will update this page first. Any cookie that is not strictly necessary to run the site will only be set with your consent, or under an exemption permitted by the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations 2003 (PECR), as amended by the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025 — for example, cookies used solely for anonymised statistical purposes, provided we give you a simple way to object.

Your choices

Because this site does not set cookies, there is nothing to manage here today. If that changes, this page will explain what is used and how to control it, and you can always manage or block cookies through your browser settings for any site.
